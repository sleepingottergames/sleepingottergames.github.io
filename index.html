Fight to the beat

Every enemy attacks to the music. Read the rhythm, defend, then strike back at full power.

As a Forcana Conductor, you will:

  • 🛡️Swipe to the beat to parry and dodge enemy attacks
  • ⚔️Tap to the rhythm to power up your sword, spear and wand
  • 🃏Build the ultimate spell deck to unleash absurdly BIG numbers
  • 🗺️Learn monster patterns and conquer tricky gimmicks across nine biomes
  • 💎Collect artifacts and master classes to find your perfect play style
  • 👗Customize your companion into your perfect chibi waifu or husbando!

Play Forcana for free, climb leaderboards, and earn bragging rights along the way!
