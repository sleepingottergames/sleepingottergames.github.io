Please include:

  • A screenshot or screen recording, if you can

Frequently asked questions

A purchase didn't arrive

Close and reopen the game with an internet connection first. If the item still hasn't arrived, email us with the date of the purchase and the receipt or order ID from your App Store or Google Play purchase history.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are processed by Apple or Google, so they handle refunds. On iOS, request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, use Google Play's refund page.

I lost my progress

Email us with your device, roughly when you last played, and what you had (companion level, weapons). We'll look into it.

How do I delete my data?

Email sleeping.otter.games@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request". Our Privacy Policy explains what we collect and your rights.
